Pattern

Empty and error states

When a region has nothing to show, say which kind of nothing it is, what will fill it, and the one thing the person can do about it.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Mission Control is empty a lot: a new project has no designs until stage 2, no backlog until stage 9, and nobody is allowed to see the audit log without being an Owner. Each of those is a different situation and gets different words, so a person never wonders whether something is broken.

Six kinds share one component, Empty state: first run, waiting, no results, forbidden, failed to load, and the full Error page. They replace the region that would hold the content (a tile body, a list, a page body) and keep everything around it: the shell, the page header and its filters stay, so the person can change course.

Copy rules build on Content and Content and tone.

Structure

Icon, title, one or two sentences, at most two actions. Left aligned in page bodies with a hairline above; centred (.empty--center) only inside small tiles.

States

One region, the Audit log, in each state it can be in. The page header and its filters stay in every state except forbidden, where filters are meaningless.

StateWhat the region showsMock

Choosing the right one

  • Check permission first, then failure, then filters, then emptiness. A Viewer on Audit sees forbidden even when the store is down.
  • No results is never shown as first run: if filters are on, say so and offer to clear them.
  • Role changes the first-run copy, not just the button: Owners get Describe a product… with New product; Viewers get When an Owner gives Hewn a direction… with no button.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below sm the icon, text and actions stack left aligned at full width; buttons stay their natural width (they never stretch).
  • .empty__text is capped at 28rem so lines stay readable on wide screens.
  • Tight empties (.empty--tight) halve the vertical padding for tiles and filtered lists; centred empties are only used inside tiles, where the tile is the frame.
  • Error pages centre in at least 70% of the viewport height and keep the 16px margin on phones.

Accessibility

Structure

The empty title is a heading one level below the page heading (h2 in a page, h3 in a tile), so it shows up in the heading list. The icon is aria-hidden: the title says it all.

Announcements

Failed-to-load states carry role="alert" because they replace content the person was waiting for. First run, waiting, no results and forbidden are quiet: they arrive with the page. When filters produce no results, the change is announced by the polite live region that wraps the results.

Touch

Actions are 40px buttons; there is never more than two, so they cannot crowd.

Content

  • Titles are short statements, not apologies: No backlog yet, Projects couldn’t load, That project isn’t here. No Oops, no exclamation marks.
  • Explain the factory: say which stage fills the region. The backlog is produced by stage 9, Estimates.; Stage 2 designs every screen and state in Magic Patterns…
  • Errors name the source and code: The Hewn API returned 503 Service Unavailable.; GitHub returned 502 while listing commits.; timeout after 10 s.
  • Then say what is still safe, because the person's first fear is lost work: Your directions are still running on their workers.; Entries are still being written; nothing has been lost.
  • Forbidden says why and who can help, without blame: …so only Owners can read it. Ask an Owner if you need an entry.
  • 404s do not leak: It may have been deleted, or it belongs to a Hewn you can’t see. Nothing about other projects is shown on purpose.
