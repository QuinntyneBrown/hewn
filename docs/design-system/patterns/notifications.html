Pattern

Notifications

Three channels, one rule: toasts for things that happened, strips for conditions that hold everywhere, callouts for the state of the thing in front of you.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Hewn streams events (sequence #48,213 and counting) to every open Mission Control. Most of them update a status or a number in place and need no notification at all. A few are worth interrupting for, and the channel depends on whether the news is an event, a condition, or the state of the object on screen.

ChannelForLivesDismissalRole
ToastEvents in projects the person may not be watching: merged, stage started, budget warning, blockedBottom right (bottom, full width, below sm), over contentInfo and success after 5 s; warning after 10 s; danger stays until dismissed. Hover and focus pause the timerstatus region; danger in a separate alert region
Strip (alert)System state that affects every page: connection, workers stopped, credentials invalid, skills updateUnder the top bar, full width, pushes content downPersists while the condition is true; success strips fade after 4 sstatus; alert for danger
Callout (alert)State of one object or the result of the person's action: paused, budget reached, saved, failedInside the page or dialog, near the objectPersists while true; never auto-dismissesstatus or alert
Status / countThe current state of an item, and how many need attentionOn the item, in the navChanges with the itemNone; text is in the item

For feedback on the person's own actions (busy buttons, saved callouts) see Feedback.

When to use

EventChannelWhy
A pull request merged, a stage startedInfo / success toastGood to know, safe to miss; the dashboard has it too
A project reaches 80% of budgetWarning toast, 10 sNeeds a decision soon, not now
A project is blockedDanger toast with Open log and Retry stage; staysWork has stopped until someone acts
A project paused at its budgetWarning callout on that project (Paused · Budget reached) + status wordIt is the state of the object, and the fix (Raise budget) belongs there
Connection lost or reconnectingStrip; sidebar and top bar stateAffects every number on every page
Workers stopped by an OwnerStrip until started againA condition that holds across pages
Credentials invalidDanger strip with Open settingsBlocks merges everywhere; only an Owner can fix it
Five events in a secondThree toasts, then +2 more in the event streamA pile of toasts is noise

Priority and stacking

  • One strip: if several conditions hold, show the most severe (danger, then warning, then info); connection outranks system strips because nothing else is trustworthy while it is down.
  • Three toasts: newest on top; older ones fold into +N more in the event stream. A danger toast never folds away.
  • Grouping: repeated events from one project replace the previous toast (stage 3 started → stage 3 succeeded) rather than stacking.
  • No duplicates: an event already shown as a callout on the current page (budget reached while on Kiln Booking's pipeline) does not also toast.

StateBehaviour

Leaving.toast--leaving: fades and drops over --duration-base with --ease-exit
DismissedRemoved; focus, if it was inside, moves to the next toast or back to where it came from

Strip lifecycle

Connection: Connecting… on load, nothing while live, Reconnecting… with backoff, Connection lost with Retry now after fast retries give up, then Reconnected for 4 s. System strips stay until their condition clears.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below sm the toast region spans the width with a 16px inset and sits above the safe area; from sm it is a --layout-toast-width column at the bottom right.
  • Strips are full width at every size; text wraps and the action drops under it when there is no room (flex-wrap).
  • On phones a visible bottom sheet hides the toast region until it closes, so toasts never cover a dialog's actions.

Accessibility

Live regions

One polite region (role="status", aria-live="polite") for info, success and warning toasts, and a separate role="alert" region for danger toasts, so nothing is announced twice. Both regions exist on load (empty) so the first toast is announced. Strips use role="status", or role="alert" for danger.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabToasts are not in the way: they follow the page in the tab order and never take focus when they appear.
Enter on a toast actionRuns it (Open log navigates; Retry stage retries and dismisses the toast).
Enter on closeDismisses; focus moves to the next toast or back to the page.
F6 (app shortcut)Moves focus to the newest toast, so keyboard users can reach a danger toast's actions.

Labelling

Close buttons name what they dismiss: Dismiss: Parcel Post is blocked. Icons are aria-hidden; the title carries the meaning.

Motion

Reduced motion keeps the fade and drops the movement; spinning strip icons stop.

Touch

The close button is 24px (--control-height-xs) with space around it; toast actions are at least 24px tall (--target-min).

Content

  • Toast titles are the event, with the project first: Ledgerline #27 merged, Tidewater started stage 3, Parcel Post is blocked.
  • Toast detail is one line of context with numbers: Split a transaction · 62% complete; $200.00 of $250.00. It pauses at $250.00.
  • Strips say the condition and its consequence in one sentence, then reassure or instruct: Connection lost. Retrying every 30 s; agents keep working and nothing is lost.
  • Say who did it when a person did: All workers stopped by quinntynebrown at 08:30.
  • Actions are short verbs that lead to the fix: Open log, Retry stage, Retry now, Open settings, Review.
