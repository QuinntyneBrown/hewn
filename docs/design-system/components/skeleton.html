Skeleton

Skeletons hold the shape of content while it loads, so the page appears at once and nothing jumps when the data arrives.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use skeletons for the first load of a page or region whose layout is known: the projects list, the pipeline, the increment, the designs list. The shell (sidebar, top bar, page header) renders for real; only the data areas are skeletons.

  • Short waits on one control: a Spinner.
  • Measurable work: a Progress bar.
  • Load failed: replace the skeleton with an error state in the same place.

Anatomy

  1. Busy regionaria-busy="true" + aria-live="polite" wrapper with a visually hidden label (Loading projects).
  2. Title bar.skeleton--title where the heading will be.
  3. Circle.skeleton--circle where the status icon or avatar will be.
  4. LinesRagged .skeleton--w* lines; every skeleton is aria-hidden.

Variants

States

Increment 7 of 12
  • Loading: a soft highlight sweeps left to right on --duration-pulse.
  • Reduced motion: --duration-pulse is 0s, so the shapes hold still.
  • Loaded: content replaces the skeleton in one swap; aria-busy is removed and the region's heading or first item is announced politely.
  • Timed out: after 10 s with no data the skeleton is replaced by the error state (Ledgerline’s stages didn’t load (timeout after 10 s)).

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons follow the layout they stand in for: the same grid, columns and stacking at every breakpoint as the loaded content (stacked rows below lg, table columns from lg).
  • Ragged widths are percentages, so lines scale with the column.
  • Never show more skeleton rows than fit the first screen: 3 rows in a phone list, 6 to 8 on desktop.

Theming

The base is --color-bg-subtle and the sweep highlight --color-bg-subtle-hover, both one step off the surface in each theme, so skeletons read as placeholders without drawing the eye.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Every skeleton is aria-hidden="true". The region that is loading carries aria-busy="true" and aria-live="polite" plus a visually hidden label, so a screen reader hears Loading projects once and then the content when aria-busy is removed. See aria-busy.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneSkeletons are not focusable; Tab skips the loading region until it has content.

Focus

If focus was inside a region that reloads, keep it on the region's heading, not on a skeleton.

Labelling

The hidden label names what is loading: Loading increment 7, Loading the design system.

Contrast

PairTokensLightDarkMinimum
Loading hint under a skeleton--color-fg-subtle on --color-bg-surfaceAaAa4.5:1
Error icon after a timeout--color-danger-icon on --color-bg-canvasAaAa3:1
Error text after a timeout--color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvasAaAa4.5:1

Motion

The shimmer is a slow, low-contrast loop on --duration-pulse (2s), well under the three-flashes threshold; under reduced motion it stops.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Skeletons have no text. The hidden label says Loading {thing}; a visible hint under a loading control says what is coming: Loading your products….
  • Do not put real headings inside a skeleton region unless they are final (the page h1 may be real and visually hidden: Increment 7).
