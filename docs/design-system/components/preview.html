Preview

Previews show the product Hewn is building: the captioned walkthrough video of each increment, and the exported mocks and design-system pages in a sandboxed frame.

Overview

Previews are pictures of someone else's product inside Mission Control. They use their own fixed --color-preview-* tokens on purpose: the built app has its own theme, so switching Mission Control to dark must not repaint the product being previewed. Only the frame around it follows the Mission Control theme.

  • Increment video (.video): the captioned 1080p walkthrough recorded once every acceptance test is green, with play, seek, captions and full screen (pages/increment/default).
  • Video placeholder (.video--placeholder): the same 16:9 box, saying why there is no video yet or why capture failed.
  • Design preview (.preview + .preview__frame): a sandboxed, separate-origin frame showing an exported mock or design-system page at a chosen width and theme (pages/designs/default).
  • State list (.state-list): the screens and states that pick what the design preview shows.

The mocks draw the product with .mini-app blocks; in the product the frame is an <iframe sandbox="allow-scripts"> on mocks.hewn.dev with no allow-same-origin, so a mock cannot read Mission Control's cookies or storage (L2-011).

Anatomy

  1. Frame.video__frame: the recorded product, in fixed preview tokens; aria-hidden because the real element is <video>.
  2. Caption.video__caption: burned-in style captions on --color-bg-scrim, from the WebVTT track.
  3. Play.video__btn: play or pause, labelled by its action.
  4. TimeElapsed / total, tabular mono.
  5. Seek.video__track: a slider with a spoken time value.
  6. Captions, full screenCaptions is a toggle (aria-pressed); full screen appears from sm.

Sizes

The design preview's width is set with --w on .preview__frame to one of the five preview widths, picked with a segmented control; the frame never exceeds its container (min(100%, var(--w))) and .preview scrolls sideways if the chosen width is wider than the screen.

Responsive behaviour

  • Base (360px): the video fills the column at 16:9; the recorded product's sidebar is hidden; full screen is hidden (phones go full screen by rotating); caption type scales between 12px and 16px with clamp().
  • From sm (36rem): the recorded sidebar and the full screen button appear.
  • From lg (62rem): on the increment page the video takes the main column of .split--aside and the pull request sits in the rail; the design preview sits beside the state list in .split--nav.
  • State list: below lg it wraps horizontally as a row of 44px links (--target-comfortable); from lg it is a vertical list of 32px links.
  • Design preview: the frame is min(100%, --w); wider choices scroll inside .preview, never the page.

Theming

Previews are deliberately not themed by Mission Control. The --color-preview-* tokens hold the same values in light and dark, because they depict the built product, and --color-preview-dark-* exist for when the person previews the product's own dark theme. The scrim tokens (--color-bg-scrim, --color-fg-on-scrim) are fixed too, because captions sit over video that can be any colour.

/* Preview a product whose accent is green: override the preview set, not the Mission Control tokens */
.preview--harbor { --color-preview-accent: var(--palette-green-700); --color-preview-mark-accent: var(--palette-green-400); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

In the product the video is a native <video> with a WebVTT <track kind="captions" default>; the custom controls are buttons and an APG slider for seek. The design preview is an <iframe> with a title (Exported mock: Reconcile statement, Review state, 992 px, light); the mocks stand it in with role="img" and the same text as aria-label. The state list is a list of links with aria-current="true".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPlay, seek, captions, full screen, then the state list links in order.
Enter, SpacePlay or pause; toggle captions; enter full screen; follow a state link.
← / →On seek: back or forward 5 seconds.
Page Up / Page DownOn seek: back or forward 10% of the video.
Home / EndOn seek: start or end.
EscLeaves full screen.

Focus

Video buttons and the seek track show the shared focus ring; on the dark scrim the ring is still --color-focus-ring with its offset, so it stays visible. Following a state link moves focus to the preview heading so the new state is announced.

Labelling

The play button's label is its next action (Play / Pause); captions is a toggle with a fixed label (Captions) and aria-pressed; seek speaks aria-valuetext="1 minute 22 of 2 minutes 14". The video has a visible caption line under it: Captioned walkthrough · 1080p · 2:14. External state icons say Open Reconcile statement · Review in Magic Patterns.

Motion

The video never autoplays and has no motion until the person presses Play. Placeholders and frames are static. Respect reduced motion in the product: do not start a preview's own animations when prefers-reduced-motion is set.

Touch

Video buttons are 32px circles with 12px between them on the control bar; the seek track is 4px tall with a 32px tall hit area (the whole bar row). State links are 44px below lg.

Content

  • Every video has captions; the caption track is written by the agent from the walkthrough script.
  • The line under the video says what it is: Captioned walkthrough · 1080p · 2:14, and where it lives: docs/videos/reconcile-card-statement/.
  • Placeholders say when the video will exist or why it does not: The walkthrough records once every test is green. Implementing, iteration 3 of 10.
  • Failure placeholders name the failure, the retry and the consequence: Video failed: browser crashed during capture … The pull request went ahead with this noted under Notes.
  • Frame titles: Exported mock: {screen}, {state} state, {width} px, {theme}.
