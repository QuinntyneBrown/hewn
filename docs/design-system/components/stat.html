Component · Data display

Stat

Stats show the numbers Mission Control is watched for: what is running and how far it has got, what it has cost today and this week, and how much of a project's budget is gone.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a stat when a number is the point. Put the figure first and large, its label and context small. Use a meter for a share of a known total (budget, percent complete) and a Table when people compare many numbers.

  • Live run (.run): a project's name, live stage, percent and spend of budget, the current step and the latest log line, in the Live work tile.
  • Spend tile (.spend + .bars): today's spend large, the week beside it, seven bars ending today.
  • KPI (.stat): label, value, optional delta with arrow and sign.
  • Project spend (.phead__spend): $184.20 of $400.00 over a meter in the project header.

Anatomy

  1. Name.run__name, --text-h2, links to the pipeline.
  2. Live stage.run__stage, accent with a spinning loader; warning ink and a pause icon when held.
  3. Money.run__money: percent, spend and of budget, tabular, pushed right.
  4. StepTwo body-sm lines: what is being built and how far.
  5. Live line.run__line: the latest log line in code type on the sunken surface, worker on the right from sm.

Sizes

Figures take the size of their context, not a modifier: --text-h1 for tile figures and KPIs, --text-h2 for a run's name, --text-body-sm in the project header.

States

Stats are read, not operated; the run's name is the only interactive part. Loading, empty and attention states come from data.

Responsive behaviour

  • Live run: head items wrap (name, stage, then money pushed right); the worker in the live line appears from sm (36rem); padding grows from --space-4 to --space-5 at sm.
  • Spend tile: figure and bars stay side by side down to 360px; the bars are fixed width (7 × 10px plus gaps).
  • Project spend: a min-width of 11rem, wrapping under the title below xl with percent complete and the controls.

Theming

Figures read --color-fg-default; context --color-fg-muted and --color-fg-subtle; the live stage --color-fg-accent; bars --color-border-grid with today in --color-accent; the live line sits on --color-bg-surface-sunken. Deltas use the warning and success fg tokens. In dark, past bars are the dark grid line and today the lighter accent; both stay visible on the dark surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text, with the meter following the APG meter pattern (role="meter", aria-valuenow/min/max and an aria-valuetext in money). The bar chart is role="img" whose label lists every day's amount. Figures are not live regions: they change several times a minute; budget warnings are announced by a toast.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the run's name link; the rest is static.
EnterOpens the project's pipeline.

Focus

The shared ring on the run's name.

Labelling

Every figure has a visible label or unit nearby (this week, of $400.00). Deltas include a sign and a comparison (−$17.30 vs yesterday); the arrow is decorative. The spend meter's value text is $184.20 of $400.00.

Motion

The live stage loader spins and the figures update in place without counting animations. Reduced motion stops the loader.

Touch

The run's name is the target (h2 text, well over 24px). Bars are not interactive; exact values are in the label and on the dashboard's spend detail.

Content

  • Money has a currency sign and two decimals: $86.40, $250.00 of $250.00.
  • Percent complete is a whole number: 62%.
  • Deltas: sign, amount, comparison: −$17.30 vs yesterday. Use the minus sign (−), not a hyphen.
  • Bar day labels are single letters ending with today; the chart's label spells them out.
  • Held runs say why and where: Paused at checkpoint · stage 10.
