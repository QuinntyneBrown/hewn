Alert

Alerts tell people about a condition that matters on this page or across the app: inline callouts sit in the content, strips run full width under the top bar.

Overview

Two forms, one job: telling someone about a condition that lasts until something changes.

  • Callout (.callout): inside the content, next to what it is about. The pipeline's Stage failed after 3 retries, the settings Defaults saved, the form error summary, the sign-in This account doesn’t have access.
  • Strip (.strip): a full-width band under the top bar for conditions that affect the whole app: connection state, all workers stopped, GitHub App credentials invalid, a skills update. Strips stay until the condition ends.

Use a Toast for passing news, an Inline message for one field, and an Empty state when the error replaces the content.

ToneCalloutStripRole
NeutralSession expired, direction cancelled.Workers stopped (in the shell).status
InfoDirection queued, read-only for Viewers.Connecting, skills update available.status
SuccessDefaults saved, direction complete, signed out.Reconnected, workers started.status
WarningPaused, budget reached, rate limited.Reconnecting, all workers stopped.status
DangerStage failed, form errors, access denied.Connection lost, GitHub App credentials invalid.alert when it appears in response to something; status for a state present on load

Anatomy

  1. IconTone icon at 20px, aria-hidden.
  2. Title.callout__title: what is true now, in one line.
  3. Body.callout__body: why, what is safe, what happens next.
  4. Actions (optional).callout__actions: small buttons or a compact form (raise budget).
  5. Dismiss (optional).callout__close: only for callouts that report a finished action.

Sizes

Callouts and strips have one size each; they take the width of their container and grow with their text.

States

The alert itself is not interactive; its buttons are. Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state.

VariantDefaultHoverFocusActive
Callout dismiss
Strip action
  • Present on load: a state the page was rendered in (Paused, Blocked, Read-only). role="status" or no role; it is read in reading order.
  • Appears in response: after a submit, a retry or a connection change. role="alert" for danger, role="status" otherwise, on an element that exists before its content is inserted.
  • Resolved: strips are removed when the condition ends (Reconnected… shows for 4 s first); callouts reporting a finished action may be dismissed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Callouts take the full width of their column at every breakpoint; actions wrap under the body.
  • Strips span the whole content area under the top bar; their side padding is --layout-margin (16px below lg, 32px from lg) so the text lines up with the page.
  • Below sm a strip's action wraps onto its own line, left-aligned under the text; from sm it sits at the end of the row (margin-left: auto).
  • Strips stack in priority order when several apply: connection, then workers stopped, then system.

Theming

Callouts read five component tokens that each tone overrides: --callout-bg, --callout-fg, --callout-ring, --callout-icon, --callout-title. Strips read the same status tokens directly. In dark the status backgrounds become deep tints and the foregrounds lighten, keeping 4.5:1.

/* A callout on a raised dialog surface keeps its tone but drops the ring */
.dialog .callout { --callout-ring: transparent; }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Callouts and strips use role="status" (polite) or, for danger that appears in response to something, role="alert", following the APG alert pattern. Alerts never take focus, with one exception: the form error summary receives focus after a failed submit (tabindex="-1"), per the forms pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the alert's links and buttons in reading order.
Enter, SpaceActivates an action; in the error summary Enter on a link moves focus to that field.

Focus

Alerts do not move focus when they appear (except the error summary). Dismissing a callout moves focus to the next focusable element after it.

Labelling

The title and body are the announcement; write them to stand alone. Icons are aria-hidden; the tone is never carried by colour alone because the title says it (Stage failed, Paused).

Motion

Alerts appear in place without animation. The reconnecting strip's icon spins on --duration-spin and stops under reduced motion; the word Reconnecting… still carries the state.

Touch

Actions are small buttons (32px) with 8px gaps; the dismiss button is 32px. Strip actions sit clear of the strip text on phones.

Content

  • Title states what is true now: Paused · Budget reached, Stage failed after 3 retries, Defaults saved.
  • Body answers: why, what is safe, what next. Retried after 30 s, 2 min and 10 min. Other projects keep running.
  • Strips are one sentence plus the next attempt or the fix: Connection lost. Retrying every 30 s; agents keep working and nothing is lost.
  • Name who did it and when: Paused by quinntynebrown at 14:29.
  • Error summary title counts: 2 things to fix; each item names the field and the problem.
