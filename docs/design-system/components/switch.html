Switch

A switch turns a setting on or off and the change takes effect immediately, with no Save button.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="switch" on a checkboxCore set: not yet in the mocks

Overview

Use a switch for a preference that applies the moment it is flipped and is saved on its own: which live events raise a toast, whether the log follows new lines. If the change waits for a Save or Apply button, use a Checkbox. If it changes the view rather than a setting (Follow log in a toolbar, Captions on the increment video), use a toggle Button with aria-pressed.

The mocks do not use a switch yet. Its first home is a Notifications section in Settings, beside Appearance, which is already saved immediately; the four live-toast kinds (merged, stage started, budget at 80%, blocked) map one switch each.

  • Label after (.switch): inline, for a single preference in a panel.
  • Label before (.switch--end): settings rows, label left and switch right-aligned, so a column of switches lines up.
  • With description: a <small> under the label says what the setting changes.

Anatomy

  1. Label--text-body-sm, --color-fg-default; names the setting.
  2. Description<small>, --text-caption, --color-fg-subtle.
  3. Track40×24 (--space-10 × --space-6), --radius-full; off: --color-bg-surface with a --color-border-strong ring; on: --color-accent.
  4. Thumb16px (--space-4); off: --color-fg-muted at the start; on: --color-fg-on-accent, moved --space-4.

Sizes

Switches have one size: a 40×24 track with a 16px thumb on a row at least --target-min (24px) tall; the label is part of the target.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Off
On

Both themes

  • On is shown by position, fill and thumb colour together, never by colour alone.
  • Saving: the switch moves at once (optimistic). If the save fails it moves back and an error toast says Couldn’t save notification settings. Budget warnings are still off.
  • Disabled is 50% opacity; the description says why (Always shown). Viewers can change their own notifications, so do not disable switches by role.

Responsive behaviour

  • .switch--end rows span the column at every breakpoint; the switch stays at the end and top-aligned while the label and description wrap.
  • Below sm the description wraps under the label; the switch never shrinks.
  • From lg, inside a .settings-section, the rows sit in the right-hand column beside the section heading.

Theming

Track and thumb read --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong, --color-fg-muted, --color-accent, --color-accent-hover and --color-fg-on-accent. In dark the on track is the lighter blue with a near-black thumb, matching the primary button, so on reads the same way in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<input type="checkbox" role="switch"> inside its <label>, following the APG switch pattern. The native checkbox gives keyboard, form and forced-colours support; the role makes it announce on and off.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabMoves to the switch.
SpaceToggles it and applies the setting.

Focus

The shared ring around the track. Toggling keeps focus on the switch; an error toast after a failed save does not take focus.

Labelling

The label names the setting, not the action: Budget warnings, not Turn on budget warnings. It is announced as Budget warnings, Toast at 80% of a direction’s budget, switch, on. Never put on/off text inside the track.

Motion

The thumb slides over --duration-base and the track fills over --duration-fast, both --ease-standard. Reduced motion zeroes both; the state still changes.

Touch

The whole row is the target (label, description and track), at least 24px and about 40px with a description. The switch never relies on dragging the thumb: a tap toggles.

Content

  • Label the thing being switched, as a noun phrase: Merged pull requests, Stage started.
  • Descriptions say what happens when it is on, in the present tense: Toast when an increment merges.
  • No Enable, Turn on or question marks.
  • Group related switches under a heading (Notifications) with one sentence saying they save immediately.
