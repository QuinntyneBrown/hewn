Timeline

Timelines tell what happened in order: the test-first steps of an increment from red to merge, and the log of decisions the agent made in your place, each with its reason and commit.

Overview

Use a timeline when order in time is the point and each entry is a step or an event with a short explanation. Use a Stepper for a fixed sequence of stages people navigate between, and a List (event stream) for high-volume live events.

  • Step timeline (.timeline): a vertical rule with a status icon per step, the step, its detail and the time. On the increment page: tests committed, red recorded, green, video, pull request, squash-merge.
  • Decision log (.decision): when and in which stage, the question a skill would have asked, the answer (after an arrow), why, and the commit that records it.

Anatomy

  1. Rule and iconA hairline on the left; each step's status icon sits on it, masked by the canvas colour.
  2. Step.timeline__t, body-sm default ink, with the status word visually hidden.
  3. Detail.timeline__d, muted: counts, reasons, what happens next.
  4. Time<time class="timeline__time"> in code type, right column; empty for steps not reached.

Responsive behaviour

  • Step timeline: the same layout at every width; the detail wraps under the step and the time stays in the right column.
  • Decision log: stacked below md (when and stage on one line above the question); from md (48rem) a two-column grid with an 8.5rem when column, the date above the stage.
  • Decision text wraps; it never truncates (the reasoning is the content).

Theming

The rule is --color-border-default; icons take their status icon tokens; the icon's mask is --color-bg-canvas, so a timeline placed on a card must repaint the mask with the card's surface. The decision answer's arrow uses --color-success-fg; questions are default ink, reasons muted.

.card .timeline__icon { background: var(--color-bg-surface); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <ol>: order matters. Each step's status is in text (visually hidden after the step) and its time is a <time datetime>. Decisions are list items whose question and answer are paragraphs. No ARIA widget; see the APG structural roles (list). The arrow before an answer is decoration and must not be read (CSS alt text: content: "→ " / "").

Motion

The Running step's loader spins and stops under reduced motion. New steps appear without animation.

Touch

Decision links are caption-size text with a 12px icon; the line is at least 24px tall and links are 16px apart.

Content

  • Steps are outcomes in the past tense or the current activity: Failing tests committed, Green after 3 iterations, Implementing · iteration 3 of 10.
  • Details give counts and the next condition: 4 failing, 0 passing; Automatic when checks pass.
  • Times are 24-hour local (14:37); the current step says now; unreached steps have no time.
  • Decisions: the question as the skill would have asked it, the answer as a short sentence, the reason as one or two sentences.
  • Decision dates are day, month and time (9 Nov, 14:21); the stage is its name (Increments).
