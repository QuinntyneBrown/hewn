Menu

A menu button opens a short list of actions for one object, such as everything you can do to a project from its row.

Overview

Use a menu when one object has three or more actions that do not all deserve a visible button, or when a row is too narrow for them on a phone. Menus hold actions (and view toggles), never navigation between pages: for that use the sidebar or Tabs. One or two actions stay visible as buttons; destructive actions in a menu open a confirmation Dialog.

The mocks have not used a menu yet. The clearest place for it is the Projects list: each row carries two icon-only actions (Archive, Delete) that are disabled while a direction runs and explain themselves only in a title. One Actions menu per row would hold Open pipeline, Pause, Raise budget, Archive and Delete, with the reason shown inside the menu. Settings' user rows (Remove) and Workers' rows (Revoke) would follow once they gain a second action.

  • With icons: an icon--sm before each label, in --color-fg-subtle.
  • With sections: .menu__label headings and .menu__sep separators group related items.
  • Danger item (.menu__item--danger): last, after a separator, ending in … because a dialog follows.
  • Checkable items: role="menuitemcheckbox" with aria-checked; a ✓ marks the checked ones.
  • Shortcuts (.menu__shortcut): only for keys the page really binds.
  • Disabled with a reason: aria-disabled items stay focusable and point to a note at the end of the menu.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA .btn with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; its name says whose actions they are.
  2. Panel.menu: --color-bg-surface-raised, hairline --color-border-default, --shadow-3, --radius-md, min 13rem, padding --space-1, --z-dropdown.
  3. Item iconicon--sm, --color-fg-subtle, decorative.
  4. Separator.menu__sep, hairline --color-border-default, role="separator".
  5. Danger item--color-danger-strong text, --color-danger-icon icon.

Sizes

Menus have one size. Items are 40px tall below lg and 32px from lg (--control-height-md / -sm); the panel is at least 13rem wide and grows with its longest item.

    Closed (aria-expanded="false", menu hidden) and open (aria-expanded="true", accent tint from the button's expanded style).

    Responsive behaviour

    • The panel is anchored to the trigger's end edge and opens below it; near the bottom of the viewport it opens above, and it never extends past the 16px page margin (max width is the viewport minus both margins).
    • Below lg items are 40px tall, so each is a comfortable phone target; from lg they tighten to 32px.
    • On phones, row menus replace the separate icon actions, so each row has one 44px trigger instead of two.
    • The menu scrolls internally if it is taller than the space available; menus over seven items should be split or turned into a page.

    Theming

    The panel reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default and --shadow-3; items read --color-fg-default, --color-fg-subtle (icons and shortcuts), --color-bg-subtle-hover, --color-danger-strong and --color-fg-accent. In dark the panel is the lightest surface on the page and keeps a hairline edge, because shadows are weak on dark canvases.

    Accessibility

    Role and pattern

    APG menu button: the trigger has aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls; the panel is role="menu" labelled by the trigger; items are menuitem or menuitemcheckbox with tabindex="-1" (focus is managed by script); list items are role="none".

    Keyboard

    KeyAction
    Enter, Space, ↓ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the first item.
    ↑ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the last item.
    ↓ / ↑Next / previous item, wrapping; disabled items are included.
    Home / EndFirst / last item.
    Type a letterMoves to the next item starting with it.
    Enter / Space on an itemActivates it and closes the menu (checkable items toggle and stay open).
    EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
    TabCloses the menu and moves focus to the next control after the trigger.

    Focus

    Opening moves focus into the menu; closing returns it to the trigger. When an item opens a dialog (Delete Harbor CRM…), focus moves into the dialog and returns to the trigger when the dialog closes. Clicking outside closes the menu without moving focus.

    Labelling

    The trigger's name says whose actions they are: Actions for Harbor CRM (visible Actions plus hidden text). The menu is labelled by the trigger. Disabled items reference their reason with aria-describedby. Item labels are verbs, with … when a dialog follows.

    Motion

    The panel rises in over --duration-fast with --ease-enter (rise-in); it closes instantly. Reduced motion removes the rise.

    Touch

    Items are 40px below lg with no gaps, and the whole row is the target. The menu opens on tap, never on hover. Tapping outside or pressing the trigger again closes it.

    Content

    • Items are verb phrases in sentence case: Open pipeline, Pause direction, Raise budget….
    • End with … when the item opens a dialog that asks for more (Delete Harbor CRM…); not for items that act at once.
    • Name the object in destructive items so it is clear in the confirmation and the audit log.
    • Order: open/view first, routine actions next, destructive last after a separator.
    • Disabled items get one sentence saying how to enable them: Cancel the running direction to archive or delete.
